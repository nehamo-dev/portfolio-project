Building products

I've spent my career at the intersection of technology and human-centered design — building AI products at Amazon, Meta, Lattice, and now at HubSpot. From shaping Alexa's voice on mobile, to growing a commerce platform at Meta, reimagining the future of work at Lattice, and accelerating sales with AI — I've led products that reach millions of users and generate billions in revenue.

I've spent my career building products and leading teams that try to make work and life a little better. Most lasting progress, I've found, comes back to the basics: clarity of thought, empathy for people, and the discipline to build what actually matters.

This site is where those ideas live — through what I build, what I write, and the conversations I have with the people I coach. Part product thinking, part leadership, part figuring it out in public.

Coaching

Work with me

I work with founders, leaders, and practitioners who want to think more clearly about their work — whether that's figuring out what to build, how to lead through change, or untangling a decision that keeps getting deferred.
