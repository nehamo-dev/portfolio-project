Coaching

I work with founders, leaders, and practitioners who want to think more clearly about their work — whether that's figuring out what to build, how to lead through change, or untangling a decision that keeps getting deferred.

I'm not a cheerleader. I ask the questions that are easy to avoid, help you see what's actually going on, and tell you the truth. Most people find that useful.

Sessions are 1:1, focused, and scheduled when you need them — not on a retainer you'll feel guilty about.
